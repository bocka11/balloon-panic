BALLOON
PANIC

Pop the balloons before they reach the spikes. Every miss costs a life.

COMING SOON TO GOOGLE PLAY

HOW TO PLAY

  • TAPBalloons float up from the city below. Tap one to pop it and score points.
  • DON'T MISSA row of spikes waits at the top. Each balloon that touches them costs one of your 3 lives. Lose them all and the game ends.
  • SURVIVEEvery 10 seconds the level rises. Balloons get faster and come in bigger groups.

Balloon types

BalloonFaster balloons are worth more points.
Gold balloonRare and quick. Worth 5 points or more.
HourglassPop it to slow everything down for a few seconds.
Extra lifeThe rarest and fastest balloon. Pop it for +1 life, up to 5.
Skull bombNever pop it. Let it burst on the spikes.

Features

  • Difficulty that ramps up the longer you last
  • Combo multiplier up to ×5 for quick pops
  • Start with 3 lives and catch rare balloons for up to 5
  • Hand-made pixel art and chiptune sounds
  • Plays offline, no account needed
  • Free to play, with optional ads

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · Applies to the Balloon Panic mobile game for Android and iOS

In short
  • Balloon Panic does not ask for your name, email or any account.
  • Your best score and settings stay on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, including your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

Who we are

Balloon Panic is developed and published by Boban Todosov, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, see Contact.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score and your sound setting in the game's own storage on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Balloon Panic is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. Some ads are optional: for example, you can choose to watch an ad to continue a run after a game over.

To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process the following information from your device:

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google processes it under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent for personalised ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform when you first open it. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on general information such as the game itself and your approximate location. You can review or change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the game's menu.

Your choices

  • Android: open your phone's Settings, go to Google, then Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: if the game asks for permission to track, you can decline. You can change this later in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You can play without watching them.
  • You can stop all data collection by the game by uninstalling it.

In-app purchases

If the game offers purchases, such as removing ads, payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Balloon Panic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising service described above, the game does not share information with third parties.

Data retention and security

We do not run servers that store player data. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies. Data saved on your device stays there until you delete the game.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online leaderboards, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
